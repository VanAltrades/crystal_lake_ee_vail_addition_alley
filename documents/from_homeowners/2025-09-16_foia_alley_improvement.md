Subject: FOIA Follow-Up Request – Alley Improvements and Maintenance Records
Dear Mr. Hammonds,
I am writing to submit a follow-up FOIA request, as my original request did not provide the information I requested under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act (5 ILCS 140). Specifically, I mentioned that “I am requesting these records in order to understand what actions led to the improvement of certain alleys in this subdivision”. While your response and documents are extremely helpful in my understanding of the resurfacing of alleys within the E.E. Vail subdivision, they provided no documentation of the actions which led the City to improve the alleys in the subdivision.
Specifically, I am seeking records regarding two matters that were not addressed in the City’s initial FOIA response:
City/Resident Actions that Improved Alleys East and West of Ellsworth Street, North of Sherman Street


Please provide ordinances, resolutions, council meeting minutes, committee meeting minutes, engineering reports, or any other City records that document the decisions and actions that caused these two alleyways to become improved (transitioned from a state of unimproved grass to improved pavement).


City Maintenance Records for the Alley East of Illinois Street, North of Sherman Street


Please provide records documenting the previous maintenance by the City (e.g., mowing, grading, or other routine maintenance work orders, schedules, or budget line items) of the alley east of Illinois Street.
Alternatively, records documenting the suspension of maintenance by the City of the alley east of Illinois Street would suffice this point’s request.


I spoke with the Mayor, the City Manager, and the City Engineer regarding these matters, and they were unable to provide details. For that reason and this information's absence from the prior response, I am renewing this FOIA request for the above records.
If any portion of the request is denied, please cite the specific exemption(s) under FOIA and release all segregable portions of otherwise exempt material.
