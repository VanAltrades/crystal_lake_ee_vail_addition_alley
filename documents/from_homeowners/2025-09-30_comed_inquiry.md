Inquiry Regarding Alley Vacation Proposal and Utility Maintenance Responsibility 
Dear ComEd Representative, 
I am writing as a homeowner in Crystal Lake, Illinois, to bring to your attention an ongoing issue 
concerning the alley behind our properties, which contains ComEd poles and service lines. The City of 
Crystal Lake has proposed vacating this alley as a public right-of-way against resident wishes, statements, 
and historic precedent. The City’s proposal would shift ongoing maintenance responsibilities to ComEd 
and/or abutting property owners. 
Currently, the alley is in a significantly overgrown and impassable condition, with brush and vegetation 
blocking access to utility infrastructure. The overgrown state of the alley is the result of the City 
intentionally halting historic maintenance services to the alley. Residents have expressed concern that this 
situation creates safety risks, an inability to access the alley by personal vehicle, and will limit timely service 
access to poles, lines, and equipment. 
I would like to ask for clarification on the following points: 
1. Notification: Has the City of Crystal Lake formally notified ComEd of its proposed vacation of the 
alley and its intent to shift maintenance responsibilities? If yes, what details has the city provided 
ComEd? 
2. Current Condition: Is ComEd aware of the current overgrown state of the alley and the associated 
accessibility and safety issues? Confirmation that the City, not ComEd,  has been historically 
responsible for alley maintenance would be helpful to share if possible. 
3. Future Maintenance & Communication: If the City vacates the alley and ComEd becomes 
responsible for its upkeep, how will ComEd manage routine maintenance of grass, brush, and 
vegetation? Additionally, how will ComEd inform residents about its maintenance plans and provide 
updates with maintenance schedules and timelines to ensure transparency? 
Given that these poles and service lines are essential to the reliability of power and communications in our 
neighborhood and our subdivision has already experienced prolonged power outages and services based on 
the City’s neglect(picture attached), I believe it is critical that ComEd is fully aware of both the City’s 
proposal and the present state of the alley. 
Thank you for your attention to this matter. I would greatly appreciate a response clarifying ComEd’s 
understanding and next steps. Please reach out to me with your comments by phone or email.
