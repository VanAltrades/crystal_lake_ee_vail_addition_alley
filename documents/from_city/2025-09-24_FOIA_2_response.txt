CITY OF CRYSTAL LAKE
REQUEST FOR PUBLIC RECORDS
NOTICE OF PARTIAL DENIAL


On 9/16/2025, the City of Crystal Lake received your written request for the inspection or copying of certain City public records, to wit:
 
"Specifically, I am seeking records regarding two matters that were not addressed in the City’s initial FOIA response:

1. City/Resident Actions that Improved Alleys East and West of Ellsworth Street, North of Sherman Street

- Please provide ordinances, resolutions, council meeting minutes, committee meeting minutes, engineering reports, or any other City records that document the decisions and actions that caused these two alleyways to become improved (transitioned from a state of unimproved grass to improved pavement).

2. City Maintenance Records for the Alley East of Illinois Street, North of Sherman Street

- Please provide records documenting the previous maintenance by the City (e.g., mowing, grading, or other routine maintenance work orders, schedules, or budget line items) of the alley east of Illinois Street.
- Alternatively, records documenting the suspension of maintenance by the City of the alley east of Illinois Street would suffice this point’s request."
 
Request. 
1. Decision on Request

Your request is approved in part and denied in part.  Those records for which your Request is denied are identified in Section 4 below.

2. Availability of Records

The Specified Records are included with this notice.
 
1. The City was unable to locate records responsive to your request at this time. The alley was created prior to 1914. Please contact McHenry County for records pre-dating the incorporation of City of Crystal Lake for these alleys.
 
2. Please see the attached maintenance record that the City has located at this time.

3. Copying Fees

No copies of the Specified Records will be provided to you until the following applicable fees have been paid.  Fees must be paid in cash, by cashier's or certified check, or by money order.

Details of Fees Charged:

Total Amount of Fees Due: $0.00

4. Notice of Partial Denial

Your Request has been denied in part.  The records for which Your Request is denied are identified below:

    Private Information

a. Responsible Official

The City's Freedom of Information Officer is the official responsible for this denial.

b. Notice of the Right to Review by Illinois Attorney General

You have the right to file a request for review of Your Request and this Notice of Denial by the Public Access Counselor of the Office of the Illinois Attorney General.  The Public Access Counselor may be contacted at 500 South 2nd Street, Springfield, Illinois 62701, public.access@ilag.gov, or by telephone at (877) 299-3642.

c. Notice of Right to Judicial Review

Under Section 11 of the Illinois Freedom of Information Act, 5 ILCS 140/11, you have the right to seek judicial review of this denial.  The City cannot advise or represent you in this matter.  You should consult your own legal counsel. 

d. Justification for Denial

Your Request is being denied with respect to the Specified Records because the Specified Records are exempt from disclosure pursuant to the Illinois Freedom of Information Act for the following reason or reasons (reference are to sections of the Act):

    The Specified Records are private information. Sect. 2(c-5), Sect. 7(1)(b)
